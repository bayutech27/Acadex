Features

Everything You Need to Run Your School

Designed for school proprietors and administrators who are tired of paperwork and want a system that just works.

Auto-generated report cards & broadsheets

Compile entire classes in minutes, not weeks. Acadex automatically generates professional report cards and broadsheets for every student, every term.

Instant report card downloads

Download clean, printable, professional report cards in seconds — ready to hand out or archive. No more manual compilation.

Results sent straight to parents on WhatsApp

Stop printing result sheets and waiting for parents to come to school. Acadex sends student results directly to parents' WhatsApp.

CBT (Computer-Based Testing) exams

Set up and run computer-based tests for your students. Modern, secure, and resistant to exam malpractice.

Student attendance register

Replace paper registers with a daily digital attendance log for every class. Track who is present, absent, or late with zero stress.

Teacher clock-in/clock-out with GPS verification

Know your staff are actually on ground. GPS-verified clock-in eliminates attendance sheet gaming and keeps everyone honest.

Fee & finance tracking

See who has paid, who owes, and how much — instantly. No more guessing or digging through old receipts.

Works offline

Built for Nigerian network realities. Acadex works offline and syncs automatically when connection is restored. Your school never stops.
